# League & market screening (out-of-sample)

Generated from walk-forward predictions; test period from 2019-07-01. Reproduce with `sportpred evaluate` then `python scripts/league_screening.py`.

* **Δ log loss** = model − closing market (negative = model better). 95% bootstrap CI in brackets.
* **Yield / CLV** = flat 1-unit bets on every selection with model EV ≥ 4%, odds 1.5–5.0, priced at the pre-closing *market average* (realistic) or *market maximum* (optimistic, often stale prices).
* **Margin** = average bookmaker over-round (the cost of betting) in that league.
